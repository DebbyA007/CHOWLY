# Photography

Forty-two of the ninety-two dishes on the card carry a photograph. The rest carry a
monogram tile: the same 76px circle, a hairline rim, and the dish's initial struck into
it in Newsreader. That is a decision, not a gap. A photograph that does not read at 76
pixels is worse than a struck initial, so every candidate was rendered at the real size,
under the real treatment, on the real ground, and thrown out if it did not clearly beat
the tile. One hundred and sixty-five candidates were sourced across two passes; forty
survived, and two photographs from the first shoot (Chapman, Zobo) carried over.

The images thrown out were thrown out for reasons a reader can check: engravings and
paintings returned for dish names, a nude torso returned for a spritz, branded bottles
whose labels are readable in frame, a subject that was not the dish, and photographs
that go to mud at 76px round.

Every photograph is served from this repository under `public/photos/`, because the
app's Content Security Policy allows images from its own origin only. Each was
downloaded from the source below, cropped square and centre-weighted by the browser, and
otherwise unedited. One CSS treatment sits over all of them so forty-odd sources read as
one shoot: `sepia(0.24) saturate(0.58) contrast(0.98) brightness(0.5)`. It is darker and
flatter than the treatment the eleven-dish card used, because the tile and the
photograph now sit in the same list and had to read as the same family of object. The
numbers behind that are in `docs/AI-LOG.md`.

The set is CC0, public domain or CC BY throughout, so nothing here asks a reader to
think about share-alike. Attribution for the CC BY images is this file, which the README
links to.

## Not chosen, for the record

The first pass queried Wikimedia Commons and took the first result that passed a licence
and size check. Sixty-one downloads, of which eighteen survived the size test, because
searching an encyclopedia for "lobster pepper soup" returns an engraving of a lobster.
The second pass queried Openverse, which indexes Flickr, kept three candidates per dish
instead of one, and filtered titles that read as illustrations. One hundred and four
downloads, twenty-two accepted.

Also passed over, from the first card: the CC0 jollof plate with coleslaw, the CC0 steak
frites with a branded plate rim inside the crop, the CC0 zobo in a tub, the bright eggs
benedict under flat light, and a hibiscus iced tea that was not zobo. Nothing from the
Lagos restaurant itself exists under an open licence, so the room is a dining room at
dusk elsewhere, chosen for its light.
