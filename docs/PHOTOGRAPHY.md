# Photography

Every photograph in CHOWLY is served from this repository under `public/photos/`, because
the app's Content Security Policy allows images from its own origin only. Each one was
downloaded from the source below, cropped square and centre-weighted (the room to
390 by 452 at two times), resized with `sips`, and re-encoded as JPEG. One CSS treatment
sits over all of them so twelve sources read as one shoot: a touch warmer and a touch
darker (`sepia(0.18) saturate(1.05) contrast(1.05) brightness(0.9)`). No other edits.

The set is CC0, public domain or CC BY throughout, so nothing here asks a reader to
think about share-alike. Attribution for the CC BY images is this file, which the README
links to. One exception is marked below and waits on a decision.

## Goat pepper soup, pending

No permissively licensed photograph of goat pepper soup found is good enough to ship at
76px round. The two that exist: a CC BY 4.0 shot by QueenSaysay where the bowl sits
under a plate of yam and reads as a dark murky bowl once cropped, and a CC0 shot by
Ubiebo that is mostly yam. The share-alike image by Halima Waziri stays in place until
one of three things is decided: accept the CC BY 4.0 crop, keep the share-alike image
with its attribution, or source a photograph of the dish directly.

## Not chosen, for the record

The first set carried eight CC BY-SA photographs. They were swapped for the set above so
the project asks no share-alike question of a reader; every swap is a straight
replacement under the same treatment. Also passed over: the CC0 jollof plate with
coleslaw, the CC0 steak frites with a branded plate rim inside the crop, the CC0 zobo in
a tub, the bright eggs benedict under flat light, and a hibiscus iced tea that was not
zobo. Nothing from the Lagos restaurant itself exists under an open licence, so the
room is a dining room at dusk elsewhere, chosen for its light.
